Layout notes

  • ESP32 sits at the top edge with its antenna hanging off the board. No copper under the antenna.
  • Power section in the top-right corner, separated from the analog signals.
  • Divider resistors and filter caps sit between the ADCs and the terminals, so sensor traces stay short.
  • All sensor terminals line the bottom edge for clean cable entry through glands.
  • J9 is a spare thermistor input for true suction; a discharge thermistor can share the pads on the next revision.

Layout notes

  • Same power section and ESP32 placement as the outdoor board, so both share one assembly process.
  • Four opto inputs in a row, each with its series resistor and pull-up.
  • J5 brings out I²C for the static pressure and humidity sensors in Phase 6; leave it unpopulated for now.

Next steps
