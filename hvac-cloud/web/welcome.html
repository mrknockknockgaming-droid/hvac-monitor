How it works

Two small sensor boards go on the system, one at the condenser and one at the air handler. They run on the equipment's own 24 VAC, so there are no batteries to replace.

1

Measure

Liquid and vapor pressure, line temperatures, supply and return air, outdoor air, and the thermostat calls, every few seconds.

2

Calculate

Fullscope works out saturation temperatures, superheat, subcooling and delta-T, and compares them with targets from the equipment's nameplate.

3

Alert

A fault that holds for several minutes raises an alert by email and phone notification, with the evidence attached. It clears itself when the readings recover.

Every alert shows its evidence

Raw measurements stay visible next to every calculated value, and diagnostics say what they saw and what it could mean. Nothing is hidden behind a score.
